This is a Robosocer bot I made with my college team for robosoccer compedition. 
its body is made from aluminium to meet weight limits(5 Kg), its has a curved metal plate to catch ball and guide it to goal, we specifically used only 2 Wheels for onspot turning and better control. 

Components : 2x 300 RPM Jhonson Gear Motor, Aluminium frame, ESP32, BTS Motor Driver, FlySky Remote Controller.
