So this is a minor experiment, When I learned that fire is actually plasma consisting or hot gas ions and electrons, I went to test this fact,

I made a simple setup 2 metal plates connected to 40kV generator (I performed this Experiment in safety, current was too low to cause any permanent damage to me) 
between those high voltage plates was camphour which was the fuel for fire.

when i turned on the switch , the flame strongy attracted towards on e side of the plate, hot gas ions being positively charged were attracted to the negative terminal of the plate which confirms that fact that fire consists of hot ions and free electrons.
