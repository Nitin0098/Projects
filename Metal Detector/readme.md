
This is a Beat Frequency oscillation metal detector that detects presence of metals.

How it works: it uses two internal oscillation circuit one has fixed resonance frequency 
and other oscillator has a detector coil which changes the frequency slightly. initially both oscillators are adjusted to same resonance frequency.
so when a metal comes close to the detector coil its inductance changes slightly and that change in inductance causes a shift in frequency, the difference between the frequency becomes beat which is amplified by NPN transistor and sent to buzzer, that buzzing noise is the sound we hear.
